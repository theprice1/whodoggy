# Data Deletion Policy

At WhoDoggy, we respect your right to control your personal data. This policy explains how you can request deletion of your data.

## How to Request Deletion

- Contact us at: <privacy@whodoggy.com>
- Or use the in-app "Delete My Data" feature (coming soon)

## Deletion Process

- We will verify your identity before processing
- Data deletion requests are processed within 30 days
- Some data may be retained for legal obligations but will be anonymized

## Questions

For questions about this policy, contact us at <privacy@whodoggy.com>.
